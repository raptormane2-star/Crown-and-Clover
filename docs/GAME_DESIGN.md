# Game Design Pillars

## Build a realm you can walk through
Progress should physically change the settlement through buildings, farms, villagers, roads, defenses and eventually a castle/town hall.

## Exploration rewards curiosity
Forests, caves, ruins and foreign kingdoms should contain resources, characters, secrets, combat and loot that feed back into realm growth.

## Cozy first, danger second
Combat and conflict add stakes, while home-building, farming and village life keep the overall experience warm and approachable.

## Original identity
The game may share the warmth and readability associated with cozy pixel-art RPGs, but its world, characters, UI, art direction and gameplay systems must remain original.

## Core loop
Gather -> Build -> Explore -> Fight -> Expand -> Rule.

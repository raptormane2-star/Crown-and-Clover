# Crown & Clover Roadmap

## v0.4 - Foundation Refactor
- Split the monolithic prototype into reusable scenes/scripts.
- Item definitions and item database.
- Stackable inventory and hotbar.
- Villager/job data and basic job production.
- Reusable HUD/inventory/dialogue UI foundation.
- Preserve v0.3 gathering, farming, building, combat, cave, ruins, day/night and save/load behavior.
- Begin migration from custom world drawing to TileMapLayer.

## v0.5 - World & Building
- TileMapLayer overworld and region streaming.
- Proper physics collisions and navigation.
- Building placement preview and validation.
- Settlement -> Village -> Town -> Kingdom progression.

## v0.6 - Adventure
- Dedicated caves/dungeons.
- Equipment, weapons, armor and loot rarity.
- Expanded enemy roster and first boss.
- Quests and dialogue choices.

## v0.7 - Realm & Diplomacy
- Multiple regions and kingdoms.
- Reputation, trade, alliances and diplomacy.
- Restorable ruined settlements.
- Shops, blacksmith economy and trade routes.
